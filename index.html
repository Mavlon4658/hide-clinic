FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is hair transplantation a painful procedure?

Hair transplantation using the FUE method is performed under local anesthesia, which helps minimize discomfort during the procedure. Sensations may vary depending on individual characteristics. Detailed information is provided during the consultation.

Do the transplanted hairs fall out?

In the first weeks after the procedure, temporary shedding of the transplanted hair (shock loss) may occur, which is considered a normal part of the hair growth cycle after transplantation. Timelines and results are individual; in most cases, the desired outcome becomes visible within 8–16 months. Detailed information is provided by the supervising physician prior to the procedure.

Are there any age restrictions for a hair transplant?

The possibility of undergoing a hair transplant is determined on an individual basis. During the consultation, the overall condition of the patient’s donor area, the areas requiring density restoration, hair growth direction, the patient’s general health, the pattern of hair loss, and medical test results are taken into account. Additional individual information may also be requested.

Is any special hair care required after the transplantation?

General aftercare recommendations for the first three months after hair transplantation (Each patient receives personalized aftercare instructions from medical specialists.)

Key recommendations (summary):

First 7–10 days
  • Do not touch or scratch the transplanted area. A video on how to remove the bandage will be provided by the supervising physician.
  • Wash your hair strictly according to the doctor’s instructions.
  • Avoid direct sunlight, heat exposure, saunas, and swimming pools.
  • Sleep with your head elevated. On the day of surgery, you may go to sleep 8 hours after the procedure.
First month
  • Use only recommended shampoos and care products.
  • Do not shave or traumatize the transplanted area.
  • Temporary shedding of transplanted hair may occur — this is normal.
Months 2–3
  • Avoid intense physical activity and excessive sweating.
  • Hair gradually begins to grow. To improve hair density, plasma therapy is recommended once a month for 6 months.
  • Continue protecting the scalp from sun exposure and overheating.
  • Follow your doctor’s recommendations and attend scheduled follow-up appointments.
